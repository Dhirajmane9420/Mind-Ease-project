Our Commitment to Institutions

  • Data Privacy & Anonymity: All student data is anonymized. Institutions receive aggregated, non-identifiable wellness reports to understand campus-wide trends, never individual data.
  • Actionable Insights: Our analytics dashboard provides a high-level overview of student well-being, helping you identify stress trends during exam periods or among specific departments.
  • Seamless Integration: MindEase can be integrated with your existing student portal, providing easy access and encouraging adoption.
  • Resource Augmentation: Our platform complements your existing counseling services by providing 24/7 first-line support and a rich library of self-help resources.
